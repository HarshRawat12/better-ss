# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Better SS to be a welcoming, useful project. Be respectful, assume good
faith, give specific and constructive feedback, and make room for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and
kept confidential where practical. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject
contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

This code applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other
spaces where someone is representing the Better SS project.
